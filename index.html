The next questions are about your daily life and what matters to you. Here is what this tool will and will not do with what you share.

This tool WILL

Listen to everything you share, without judgement
Produce two personal reports that belong entirely to you: one about your home, one about your wellbeing

Give you a clean exit at any moment, with nothing stored

This tool WILL NOT

Share anything with anyone unless you press a button that says so, in plain language
Store your conversation after you close this tab
Tell OBOS anything about your health, your feelings, or your personal life
Tell your GP anything about your home or building
Make any decision about you, for you
